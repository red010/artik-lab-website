# Quotes and price lists from mail and files - public LLM dossier

This public dossier extends the Operational start page without adding promises beyond the HTML page. It is not a course. It is not software. The ERP is not touched.

An updated quote or price list is needed, with attached sheets, without whoever sells recopying from three folders. The job — negotiating, closing, entering the order — stays with the person. What is lightened is the work around it: gathering from mail and files, assembling, flagging gaps.

## When it is not suitable

- The «quote» is a take-off from drawings (PriMus, AutoCAD) or a site-hour estimate: that is the job.
- Negotiation, closing a discount, or a bid pricing strategy is required.
- The order or confirmation must be written into the ERP.
- The request arrives once or twice a year.
- Price lists and sheets do not exist, or exist only in one person's head and cannot be exported.
- An assistant that sends the quote on its own is requested.

## Search intents

- quotes from email
- price lists and sheets from PDFs
- draft quote from mail

## Need signals

- Quote requests arrive by mail with messy attachments.
- Whoever sells spends time copying lists and sheets from several folders.
- The ERP click stays with the person: the draft before that click is missing.
