Corporate AI training

Corporate AI courses: choose the right path

Artificial intelligence creates value when it enters daily work with method: documents, recurring decisions, handoffs between functions, quality controls and operational responsibilities.

Artik Lab courses are not a generic catalog of AI lessons. They are paths designed around the client's processes, built to turn individual use of AI tools into governable, measurable and repeatable company practices.

Request a company path Explore the DTR method

Core path

Start from AI Workflow Redesign Lab

For most companies, the first issue is not choosing an AI tool. It is understanding which activities should be redesigned, which data can be used, which risks need governance and which capabilities must remain inside the company.

AI Workflow Redesign Lab All courses

Recommended path

A concrete progression: redesign, skills, labs, governance.

  1. 1Redesign

    AI Workflow Redesign Lab maps real workflows and decides where AI makes sense.

  2. 2Align

    Managing AI and AI Literacy create shared language across roles and functions.

  3. 3Apply

    Vertical labs bring AI into documents, sales, operations, HR, quality and customer service.

  4. 4Govern

    Governance, secure AI and output quality turn adoption into controlled practice.

DTR method

Training adapted to the company context, not standard lessons.

Dynamic Training Rework is Artik Lab's proprietary method: the path is not identical from the first to the last session, but is recalibrated around processes, roles, materials and priorities that emerge with participants.

Companies are flooded with generic AI training, often full of examples far from real work. DTR avoids that waste and turns advanced AI tools into practices people can actually use.

Exercises on real work

Offers, reports, tickets, procedures, product sheets, technical documents and approval flows close to the client context.

Dedicated materials

Manuals, operating instructions and examples are written for the company, not recycled from standard slides.

Report after each session

Every meeting leaves a summary of needs, recurring questions, risks and processes to explore.

Final value map

The company keeps skills, priority workflows and a concrete roadmap for adoption.

How to choose the right path

How to choose the right path

Start well

If the company has no AI workflow map yet, start from Workflow Redesign Lab.

Train everyone

If a shared base is needed, choose AI Literacy or Managing AI.

Solve a problem

If the need is concrete, choose the vertical lab closest to the process.

Scale safely

If use is growing, add governance, secure AI and output quality.

FAQ

FAQ

Why start from AI Workflow Redesign Lab?

Because before introducing tools or automation the company needs to understand which workflows have real potential, which data can be used and which controls are necessary.

Are courses standard or customised?

The structure is stable, but content, examples, exercises and priorities are adapted to the client's processes.

Is programming required?

No for introductory, managerial and operational paths. It is required only in technical paths.

What remains after the course?

Operating materials, context-specific examples, usage criteria and a view of high-potential workflows.

Contact

A short conversation is enough to understand where to start.
